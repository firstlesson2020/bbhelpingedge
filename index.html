OUR STORY

From Inspiration
to Action

BB Helping Edge is a community-driven initiative inspired by the ideals of Bhagat Singh — courage, service, responsibility, and the belief that meaningful change begins with action.

We aim to turn inspiration into practical support through social service initiatives, encouraging people to participate and contribute to a better tomorrow.
