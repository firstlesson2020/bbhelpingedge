OUR STORY

From inspiration
to action.

BB Helping Edge is a community initiative inspired by the ideals associated with Bhagat Singh — courage, responsibility, service and the willingness to act for a better society.

Our purpose is simple: to bring people together and turn those values into practical social service. We organise community initiatives, contribute essential items and encourage people to participate with whatever they can — time, resources or support.

We plan social-service initiatives around key remembrance days and continue building a culture where helping others becomes an action, not just an intention.
