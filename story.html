OUR STORY

FROM INSPIRATION TO ACTION

There are some names we grow up hearing.

And then there are names that make us stop and think about what we are doing with our own lives.

For us, Bhagat Singh is one of them.

His life and courage have inspired generations. We don't see remembrance as something that should end with a photograph, a speech, or a day on the calendar.

We believe remembrance becomes meaningful when it becomes action.

That is where BB Helping Edge began.

We wanted to create something simple: a community where people could come together and do something useful for others.

Not everyone can contribute in the same way.

Some can donate.

Some can volunteer.

Some can provide materials.

Some can bring their skills.

Some can simply help spread the word.

All of it matters.

Our goal is to create opportunities for people to participate in community service and gradually build a culture where helping others becomes something we do, not just something we talk about.

TWO DATES. ONE PURPOSE.

23 MARCH

A day to remember Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, and Sukhdev, and reflect on the sacrifices of India's freedom struggle.

For BB Helping Edge, it is also an opportunity to turn remembrance into service.

27 SEPTEMBER

Our annual community initiative around this occasion: bringing people together through an act of service.
